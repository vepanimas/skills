# What to look for

Search with these. Report only what would change what gets built. Do not walk this list in the
reply, and do not treat it as an output outline.

## Missing requirements

- Who is this for, in what situation, and what are they trying to do?
- What fails, slows down, or becomes risky today, and what do people do instead?
- Is a request written as if it were the problem?
- Which boundaries will readers assume: non-goals, teams, phases, first run, empty, or failure?
- Where the document is specific enough that two teams could build different behavior, are the
  actor, trigger, outcome, and failure stated?

## Vague statements

- Are `users`, `many`, `often`, `significant`, `fast`, `intuitive`, `reliable`, `seamless`,
  `easy`, or `supported` doing work the document never defines?
- Which exact words would make two capable teams implement different behavior?

## Contradictions

- Does the summary describe a different problem, scope, or result from the body?
- Do the goals, non-goals, requirements, and success criteria disagree?
- Is a constraint written as a user need, or a user need written as an implementation choice?

## Outside the document

Look for one specific idea the document is silent on that would change who it serves, the approach,
or the scope. Do not brainstorm.
